Privacy Policy — Baby Learning World

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Baby Learning World ("the app") is a learning game for young children, made by Priyansh Bhalia ("we").

Summary

The app does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information about children or adults. It has no ads, no chat, no accounts, no in-app purchases and no links to websites, and it works completely offline.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for or collect names, ages, photos, voice recordings, location, contacts, device identifiers or any other personal information.

Information stored on the device

The app saves a few settings and the child's level progress, only on the device itself:

  • the app language (English),
  • whether sound is on or off,
  • whether "calm mode" (less animation) is on or off,
  • the voice tone chosen by a parent (cheerful, gentle or calm),
  • which game levels have been completed (no names, scores or times).

These settings never leave the device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Permissions

  • iPhone: the app requests no permissions.
  • Android: the app asks for no permissions that need your approval. It includes a few standard system permissions added by its app framework: to control audio playback, to keep the device awake while sound plays, and to check whether a network is available. It does not use the internet, camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage or the advertising ID.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking services, and shares no information with any third party. Spoken words are produced by the phone's own built-in text-to-speech voice.

Parental controls

The settings screen is protected by a parental gate (a simple sum) so that young children cannot change settings by accident.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for children under 5 and is built so that no personal information is collected from them, in line with children's privacy rules such as COPPA (United States), GDPR (European Union) and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act.
